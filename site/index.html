Quote accuracy

Quotes are checked against your transcript

Every quote is compared to the transcript before it reaches you. If it isn't an exact sentence, it isn't used.

  • Exact match only. A quote is kept only if it appears word for word in the transcript.
  • Fallback build. If the AI pass paraphrases, that piece falls back to the deterministic build.
  • Easy to audit. The sample package is unedited, so you can compare it with the source.
Kept Calendars beat to-do lists because a to-do item is a wish while a calendar block is a promise. Matches a sentence in the transcript exactly.
Rejected Calendars are better than to-do lists because they turn wishes into promises. Paraphrase. Not found in the transcript.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are the quotes real?

Yes. Every quote in every artifact is checked as an exact sentence from your transcript. If the AI-enhanced pass paraphrases, that artifact falls back to the deterministic build, so nothing is made up.

Do I need an account?

No. The free tools (show notes, titles, discussion kit, email series) work without signup, and the app is open during the beta.

Can I use audio or a transcript?

Both. Upload an audio file, which is compressed in your browser before upload, or paste a transcript from any transcription app.

What does it cost?

Free during the beta: 3 episodes a month. Pro will be $19/mo for 12 episodes with no attribution. The first 50 shows can lock in Pro for life with a one-time $49 founding membership.

Who owns the content?

You do. Everything is editable and exportable as Markdown or JSON, and nothing is published without you.
