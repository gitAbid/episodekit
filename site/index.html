FAQ
Are the quotes real?

Yes — architecturally. Every quote in every artifact is validated as an exact word-for-word sentence from your transcript; if the AI-enhanced pass paraphrases, that artifact falls back to the deterministic build. Nothing invented.

Do I need an account?

No. The free tools (show notes, titles, discussion kit, email series) work with zero signup, and the app is open while in beta.

Audio or transcript?

Both. Upload an audio file — it's compressed in your browser before upload — or paste a transcript from any transcription app.

What does it cost?

Free while in beta: 3 episodes/month. Pro will be $19/mo (12 episodes, no attribution). The first 50 shows can lock Pro for life with a one-time $49 founding membership.

Who owns the content?

You do. Everything is editable and exportable (Markdown/JSON), and nothing is published without you.
