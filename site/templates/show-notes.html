What makes good show notes (the 5 rules)

1 · Title = promise

"Focus is a budget" beats "Episode 47: Productivity". Search and humans both reward specificity.

2 · Quote only what was said

If you quote the episode, quote it word-for-word. (This is exactly what our generator enforces — AI tools inventing "quotes" is the #1 host complaint.)

3 · Timestamps are promises

Each one should earn a click: a claim, a story, a number — never "we continue talking about…"

4 · TL;DR before contents

Give the skim-reader the payoff first; the structure serves them after.

5 · Cut everything generic

If a sentence could belong to any podcast, delete it.
